# Monthly swing Fib retracement — yearly charts

- Daily: `/home/tester/hsm/potions/mnq/mnq_daily.csv`
- Fib from **confirmed monthly swing low** to **swing high**; level = `H − 0.618×(H−L)` (retracement from the high).
- **Bullish context:** month of pivot **high** closes **above prior month close**.
- Monthly pivots: **1** left / **1** right bars (fractal).
- **Green vertical line:** first daily bar **after** pivot confirmation where `low ≤ fib ≤ high`.
- **Weekly ATR Supertrend** on daily series (cyan `up`, orange `down`).
- **Yearly opening range:** Jan–Mar high/low for each chart year.
- Events CSV: [`events.csv`](events.csv)
