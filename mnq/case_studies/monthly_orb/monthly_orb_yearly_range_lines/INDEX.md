# MNQ monthly OR rails by year

Daily candles sit behind each month's opening-range high/low rails. When `mnq_monthly_orb_overlap_range_breakout.csv` is present, **overlap-cluster** trades are drawn: lavender band = combined cluster range; **orange dashed** = stop; **green dashed** = target; triangle = entry, red X = exit.
