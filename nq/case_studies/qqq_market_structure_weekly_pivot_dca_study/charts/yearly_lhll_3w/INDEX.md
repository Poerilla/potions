# QQQ Weekly L-H-LL Yearly Charts

These charts visualize the **3-week confirmed pivot** version of the weekly-pivot market-structure DCA study.

Variant shown: **`signal_static_full_window`**.

Window: **2000-01-07 through 2026-06-02**.

Markers:

- Green triangles: confirmed weekly pivot lows.
- Orange triangles: confirmed weekly pivot highs.
- Purple line: weekly low -> high -> lower-low sequence.
- Black/yellow star: next available daily open after the weekly signal is known.
- Blue square: year-end catch-up buy at the final December weekly high.

Charts are grouped by the signal's **buy year**. If a setup began in a prior year, the chart includes that earlier context.
