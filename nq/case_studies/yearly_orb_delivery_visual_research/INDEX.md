# NQ yearly ORB delivery visual research

This folder is visual research only. It separates the delivery-state marks from the performance backtest.

Markers:
- Yellow triangle: first yearly ORB breakout after Jan-Mar.
- Green triangle/circle pair: daily close broke a confirmed swing high above the yearly range.
- Red triangle/circle pair: daily close broke a confirmed swing low below the yearly range.
- Blue diamond on scale-in charts: add-on signal close.
- Purple pentagon on scale-in charts: add-on fill.
