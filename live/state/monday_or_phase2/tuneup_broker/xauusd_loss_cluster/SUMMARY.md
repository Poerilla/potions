## Practical read

- Not a single “always skip month” story — Jul/Sep/Dec are soft, but losses also hit fat-tailed bad weeks year-round.
- Soft multi-year patches are **chop / digestion after big gold runs** (2018–19, 2021–22), not a fixed calendar season.
- **Bearish 60d** is the weakest trend bucket by net; **bull-year** regimes still hold most loss dollars because that is when heat (and sitout) fire.
