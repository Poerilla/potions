StrategyPlugin Engine + PaperBroker equity / unit fills.

**Starting capital (fixed):**
- **USDJPY** = **$100,000**
- **XAUUSD** = **$250,000**

**Methodology:**
- Account equity = starting capital + cumulative strategy PnL.
- Year **PnL** = end-of-year strategy equity − prior year-end.
- Year **Gain%** = year PnL / **starting capital** (constant; comparable across years).
- Year **Gain% on eq** = year PnL / year-start account equity (compounded).
- **DD%** = year-local stress DD / starting capital (also shown on year-start equity in CSV).
- **CAGR%** = `(1 + net/capital)^(1/years) − 1` (partial years 2003/2026 day-annualized).
- **Sharpe / Sortino** from daily PnL / starting capital (252d).
- **Calmar** = year net / |year stress DD| (same as N/S).
- USDJPY cores: `*_skip_augsep`; gold: `xauusd_m2_s2_r3_tuneup`.
