# EURUSD Monday OR — fade failed breakout (15m)

## Rules

- **Monday OR:** Monday high / Monday low.
- **Arm:** Tuesday → Friday NY.
- **No breakout entry.** Signal on close beyond Monday high/low.
- **Fade** opposite when price re-enters the range by **>30% of R** (long-break → short @ Mon high − 30%R; short-break → long @ Mon low + 30%R).
- **Size:** 3 units — drop **2** @ **30%** DD, cut last **1** @ **50%** DD (no runner past 50%).
- **SL = 1R**, **TP = 2R** from fade entry. Max **2** trades/week.
- Fee $1.50/unit round-turn, PV $100k.
