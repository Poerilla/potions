# EURUSD monthly candles + prior-quarter H/L

Monthly OHLC from daily Histdata EURUSD. Quarters are shaded; dashed lines are the **previous quarter high/low**. Green ▲ = **3 higher lows** in a row; red ▼ = **3 lower highs** in a row (marked on the completing month).

- History: **2003-05-31 → 2026-03-31** (275 months)
- Layout: **4-year** chunks only (candles use day-unit body width).
