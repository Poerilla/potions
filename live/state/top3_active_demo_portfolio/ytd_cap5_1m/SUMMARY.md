## Rule

- Paper equity starts at $100,000 and compounds by NY calendar year.

- When YTD >= 5% of year-start paper equity, all open units are flattened at that minute's close (1 tick adverse + fee) and every later entry that year is blocked. No downside limit.
- `cap_fixed` keeps the published research lot sizes. `cap_scaled` scales lots by year-start paper / $100k.
