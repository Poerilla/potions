## Rule

Paper equity starts at $100,000 and compounds by calendar-year realized PnL on `equity_curve.csv`. A year stops after the first daily close that is already through 10, 15, or 20 percent of that year's starting equity. That day's PnL is kept. Later days in the calendar year are flat. The hurdle is rebuilt from the stopped equity, so a lock changes the next year's dollar target.

This is an end-of-day lock. It does not assume an intraday flatten exactly at the percentage. Stress is the published year-local intrabar peak-to-trough, cut off after the lock session. Years that never print the hurdle keep the published stress.

Calendar-year net matches the published entry-year net except where a trade's session and its realized mark fall in different years. Max |year net| gap vs the published board is $1864.82. Total net matches. Max |stress| gap is 0.0000.
