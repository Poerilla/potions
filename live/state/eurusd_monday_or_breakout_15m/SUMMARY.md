# EURUSD Monday OR breakout — 15m

## Rules

- **Monday OR:** Monday high / Monday low (NY calendar Monday).
- **Arm / entries:** Tuesday 00:00 → Friday end NY (no day filter).
- **Long:** 15m **close** > Monday high; **Short:** 15m close < Monday low.
- **R** = Monday high − Monday low; **SL = 1R**, **TP = 2R** from entry close.
- **Size:** 3 units — drop **2** at **30%** DD, cut last **1** at **50%** DD.
- If cuts never fire, open units exit together at target, week-end, or full stop.
- Same-bar stop first. Max **2** trades/week, one position at a time.
- Fee $1.50/unit round-turn, PV $100k.
