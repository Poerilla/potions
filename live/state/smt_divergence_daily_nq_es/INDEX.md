# NQ vs ES Daily SMT Divergence Study

Pivot-based SMT scan on Daily bars:

- Bearish SMT: one index makes a higher high while the other fails to make a higher high.
- Bullish SMT: one index makes a lower low while the other fails to make a lower low.

- Total divergences: **284**
- Bearish highs (red dots): **156**
- Bullish lows (green dots): **128**
- Windows (6 months each): **32**
