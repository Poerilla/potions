# YEARLY ORB Divergence Study (NQ vs ES)

Rules:

- Divergence: one market breaks OR boundary while the other fails to break the same side within 2 days.
- Red dots: divergent high breakout (upside break without confirmation).
- Green dots: divergent low breakout (downside break without confirmation).

- Total divergences: **13**
- Divergent highs (red): **9**
- Divergent lows (green): **4**
- Fakeout rate (lookahead 5 days): **69.23%** (9/13)
